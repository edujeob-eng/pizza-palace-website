About Pizza Palace

Our Story

Pizza Palace opened in 2022 as a small family-run restaurant focused on quality pizza recipes. We use fresh local ingredients each day.

It was initially founded by the british colonizers in 1965 but was later abandoned after Nigeria's indepence .Years later it was rennovated

By a rich family and now is currently headed by the CEO Sir Benedict Eduje and has become one of the best restaurants in Nigeria.

Our Mission

To deliver tasty, affordable well-made pizza and friendly dining experience to every guest.

Our Vision

To be the best pizza dining brand and overall restaurant in Nigeria and potentially in the world.
